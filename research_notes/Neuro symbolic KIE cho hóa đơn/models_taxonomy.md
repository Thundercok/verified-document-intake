# Taxonomy and State of the Art of Document AI / KIE Models (2022–2026), focus on invoices and receipts

Verification note (applies to every section): on 2026-11-21, titles, first authors, submission dates and arXiv "comments" venue strings were checked with one arXiv API query. In the venue column, "(arXiv comment)" means the venue string comes from the authors' own arXiv comment field. "Venue not verified" means the comment field was empty and no other primary source was checked. Scores come from the paper itself, or from a later paper's baseline table where that table is named. Scores from different papers often use different splits, OCR inputs and metric definitions (entity-level SER F1, TED accuracy, QA-style ANLS, field micro-F1), so they are **not directly comparable across rows** unless they sit in the same table.

## Q1. Per-model taxonomy: venue, architecture idea, OCR dependence, reported scores

### Takeaway
The field moved in three steps. In 2022–23, OCR-based layout encoders (LayoutLMv3, ERNIE-Layout, GeoLayoutLM, UDOP, DocFormerv2) reach about 97–98 entity-F1 on CORD and 88–93 on FUNSD SER after fine-tuning. OCR-free end-to-end models such as Donut and Pix2Struct were simpler and faster but clearly weaker (Donut CORD F1 84.1). In 2024–25, high-resolution general MLLMs closed the DocVQA gap and passed fine-tuned encoders (Qwen2.5-VL-72B DocVQA test ANLS 96.4 vs. 87.84 for DocFormerv2-large). On entity-level KIE benchmarks (CORD/SROIE/FUNSD), however, published zero-shot MLLM numbers are still far below fine-tuned encoders, or are not reported at all.

### Cited Findings

#### (a) OCR-based, layout-aware encoders (need an external OCR: text + boxes, ± image)
- **LayoutLMv3**: "LayoutLMv3: Pre-training for Document AI with Unified Text and Image Masking", Yupan Huang et al., ACM Multimedia 2022 (arXiv comment), arXiv:2204.08387. It uses unified text and image masking over OCR text, 2D positions and image patches. Needs OCR. In the GeoLayoutLM table, LayoutLMv3-LARGE (357M) scores FUNSD SER F1 92.08, FUNSD RE 80.35 (re-implemented), CORD SER 97.46 — [GeoLayoutLM, arXiv:2304.10759](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2304.10759). DocFormerv2's tables list LayoutLMv3-large CORD as 96.78 with word boxes vs. 97.46 with OCR lines, FUNSD 82.53 (word boxes), and DocVQA test ANLS 83.4 — [DocFormerv2, arXiv:2306.01733](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.01733). Metadata: [arXiv API record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08387).
- **LayoutXLM** (background, 2021): "LayoutXLM: Multimodal Pre-training for Multilingual Visually-rich Document Understanding", Yiheng Xu et al., arXiv:2104.08836, 2021. arXiv comment: "Work in progress". It introduced the XFUND benchmark (zh, ja, es, fr, it, de, pt). Needs OCR. LayoutXLM's XFUND averages, as reported in the LiLT paper: language-specific SER 0.8056 / RE 0.6432; zero-shot transfer from FUNSD SER 0.5561 / RE 0.4388; multitask SER 0.8201 / RE 0.7823 — [LiLT, arXiv:2202.13669](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.13669).
- **LiLT**: "LiLT: A Simple yet Effective Language-Independent Layout Transformer for Structured Document Understanding", Jiapeng Wang, Lianwen Jin, Kai Ding, ACL 2022 Main (arXiv comment), arXiv:2202.13669. It uses a dual-stream design: a light layout flow joined to any off-the-shelf text encoder (RoBERTa / InfoXLM) through bi-directional attention complementation (BiACM). It is pre-trained only on English IIT-CDIP and transfers across languages by swapping the text encoder. Needs OCR. Reported results: XFUND language-specific SER avg 0.8251 / RE 0.6781; zero-shot SER 0.6061 / RE 0.4930; multitask SER 0.8585 / RE 0.8125; FUNSD SER 0.8841 (RoBERTa); CORD 0.9607 (DocFormer is higher at 0.9633); EPHOIE 0.9797 — [LiLT](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.13669).
- **ERNIE-Layout**: "ERNIE-Layout: Layout Knowledge Enhanced Pre-training for Visually-rich Document Understanding", Qiming Peng et al., EMNLP 2022 Findings (arXiv comment), arXiv:2210.06155. It adds a reading-order serialization (layout-knowledge) and a spatial-aware disentangled attention. Needs OCR. The abstract claims new SOTA on FUNSD, CORD and Kleister-NDA, competitive results on SROIE, and a 7.98% FUNSD gain over the previous best — [arXiv:2210.06155](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.06155). A search summary gave "DocVQA ANLS 0.9627 vs DocFormer-large 0.9550". This looks like a mis-attribution, because those values sit in the CORD-F1 range, and it was **not verified**. Do not use it.
- **GeoLayoutLM**: "GeoLayoutLM: Geometric Pre-training for Visual Information Extraction", Chuwei Luo et al., CVPR 2023 Highlight (arXiv comment), arXiv:2304.10759. It is a two-stream vision + text-layout model with co-attention. Three geometric pre-training tasks (direction/distance, direction exceptions, triplet collinearity) are used, and relation heads are pre-trained to close the pre-train/fine-tune gap for relation extraction. Needs OCR. Results (399M): FUNSD SER 92.86 / RE 89.45; CORD SER 97.97 / RE(KV) 100.00 / RE(GRP) 99.45. No XFUND numbers are reported — [GeoLayoutLM](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2304.10759).
- **UDOP**: "Unifying Vision, Text, and Layout for Universal Document Processing", Zineng Tang et al., CVPR 2023 (arXiv comment and [CVF open access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Tang_Unifying_Vision_Text_and_Layout_for_Universal_Document_Processing_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf)), arXiv:2212.02623. It is a generative encoder-decoder (794M) over unified vision-text-layout tokens, with a layout-induced vision-text representation. Needs OCR (OCR lines). In the DocFormerv2 tables: FUNSD 91.62 (OCR lines), CORD 97.58 (OCR lines), DocVQA test ANLS 84.7, InfoVQA 47.4 — [DocFormerv2](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.01733). The CVPR version claims first place on the DUE-Benchmark leaderboard (7 tasks) and SOTA on CORD — [CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Tang_Unifying_Vision_Text_and_Layout_for_Universal_Document_Processing_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf).
- **DocFormer / DocFormerv2**: "DocFormerv2: Local Features for Document Understanding", Srikar Appalaraju et al. (AWS AI Labs), arXiv:2306.01733, 2023. The venue was not verified: the arXiv comment is empty, and AAAI 2024 is commonly cited but was not confirmed here. It is an encoder-decoder over image, OCR text and boxes, with a light linear visual branch. The encoder gets asymmetric pre-training (token-to-line, token-to-grid) and the decoder gets denoising MLM. Needs OCR. Results: FUNSD F1 88.89 (large, 750M) / 88.37 (base); CORD F1 97.70 (large) / 96.80 (base); DocVQA test ANLS 87.2, or 87.84 with extra VQA data; InfoVQA 48.8. DocFormer (v1, ICCV 2021, background) is listed at FUNSD 84.55 and CORD 96.99 (large) — [DocFormerv2](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.01733).

#### (b) OCR-free end-to-end vision-language models (pixels in, text/JSON out, small task-specific models)
- **Donut**: "OCR-free Document Understanding Transformer", Geewook Kim et al. (NAVER CLOVA), ECCV 2022 (arXiv comment), arXiv:2111.15664. It pairs a Swin encoder with a BART decoder and is pre-trained with synthetic text reading (SynthDoG). No OCR. Results from the v5 updated Table 2 (143M): CORD F1 84.1 / TED-acc 90.9 at 1.2 s/img; Ticket 94.1 / 98.7; Business Card (Japanese) 57.8 / 84.4; Receipt (Korean, in-house) 78.6 / 88.6. In the same table, LayoutLMv2 + CLOVA OCR scores CORD 78.9 / 82.4 at 1.7 s (+OCR params). DocVQA test ANLS 67.5 (Donut) vs. 78.1 (LayoutLMv2+MS OCR) and 86.7 (LayoutLMv2-Large-QG). Speed was measured on a P40 GPU — [Donut](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2111.15664). Caveat: Donut's CORD F1 here is a field-level tree F1 under Donut's own evaluation. It is not the token/entity SER F1 (around 97) used for LayoutLM-family results.
- **Pix2Struct**: "Pix2Struct: Screenshot Parsing as Pretraining for Visual Language Understanding", Kenton Lee et al., ICML 2023 (arXiv comment "Accepted at ICML"), arXiv:2210.03347. It is pre-trained by parsing masked web screenshots into simplified HTML, with variable-resolution input. No OCR. DocVQA test ANLS 72.1 (base) / 76.6 (large, 1.3B); InfoVQA 38.2 / 40.0 — [DocOwl 1.5 Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12895).
- **Nougat**: "Nougat: Neural Optical Understanding for Academic Documents", Lukas Blecher et al. (Meta AI), arXiv:2308.13418, 2023. ICLR 2024 is commonly cited, but the venue was not verified (arXiv comment: "17 pages, 10 figures"). It is a Donut-style model for converting academic PDFs to markup. No OCR. It reports no CORD/SROIE/FUNSD/DocVQA numbers in what was checked, and it is not a KIE model — [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.13418).
- **Dessurt**: "End-to-end Document Recognition and Understanding with Dessurt", Brian Davis et al., arXiv:2203.16618, 2022. Venue not verified (often cited as ECCV 2022 Workshops). No OCR. DocVQA ANLS 63.2 — [DocOwl 1.5 Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12895); FUNSD F1 65.0 (127M) — [DocFormerv2 Table 5](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.01733).

#### (b′) OCR-free document MLLMs (LLM decoder + high-resolution visual encoder)
- **UReader**: "UReader: Universal OCR-free Visually-situated Language Understanding with Multimodal Large Language Model", Jiabo Ye et al., arXiv:2310.05126, 2023. Venue not verified (often cited as EMNLP 2023 Findings). It uses shape-adaptive cropping on mPLUG-Owl and fine-tunes only 1.2% of parameters (abstract). Scores (7.1B): DocVQA 65.4, InfoVQA 42.2, DeepForm 49.5, KLC 32.8 — [DocOwl 1.5 Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12895).
- **mPLUG-DocOwl (v1) / 1.5 / 2**: "mPLUG-DocOwl 1.5: Unified Structure Learning for OCR-free Document Understanding", Anwen Hu et al., arXiv:2403.12895, 2024 (venue not verified). It uses an H-Reducer, a 1×4 conv that merges horizontally adjacent visual tokens (1,024 → 256 per 448² crop, up to 9 crops), plus structure-aware parsing and multi-grained text localization (DocStruct4M). No OCR. DocOwl-1.5 (8.1B): DocVQA 81.6, InfoVQA 50.4, DeepForm 68.8, KLC 37.9. DocOwl-1.5-Chat: DocVQA 82.2. The original DocOwl (7.1B): DocVQA 62.2, DeepForm 42.6, KLC 30.3 — [DocOwl 1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12895). "mPLUG-DocOwl2: High-resolution Compressing for OCR-free Multi-page Document Understanding", Anwen Hu et al., arXiv:2409.03420 (venue not verified; its scores were not retrieved) — [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03420).
- **TextMonkey**: "TextMonkey: An OCR-Free Large Multimodal Model for Understanding Document", Yuliang Liu et al., arXiv:2403.04473, 2024 (venue not verified). The abstract reports +5.2% on scene-text tasks, +6.9% on document tasks (DocVQA, InfoVQA, ChartVQA, DeepForm, KLC, WTQ), +2.8% on KIE tasks (FUNSD, SROIE, POIE), and OCRBench 561 — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04473). Absolute per-dataset KIE scores were not retrieved.
- **Monkey / DocPedia / CogAgent / Qwen-VL** (for context, same table): DocVQA 66.5 / 47.1 / 81.6 / 65.1 — [DocOwl 1.5 Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12895).

#### (c) General multimodal LLMs
- **Qwen2.5-VL**: "Qwen2.5-VL Technical Report", Shuai Bai et al. (Qwen Team), arXiv:2502.13923, Feb 2025. Native-resolution ViT, no OCR needed. DocVQA test 96.4 (72B) / 95.7 (7B) / 93.9 (3B); InfoVQA 87.3 / 82.6 / 77.1; OCRBench 885 / 864 / 797; CC-OCR 79.8 / 77.8 / 74.5; OCRBench_v2 en/zh 61.5/63.7 (72B). The same Table 5 gives: Claude-3.5 Sonnet DocVQA 95.2, OCRBench 788; Gemini 1.5 Pro DocVQA 93.1, OCRBench 754; GPT-4o DocVQA 91.1, OCRBench 736; InternVL2.5-78B DocVQA 95.1, OCRBench 854. The abstract claims "robust structured data extraction from invoices, forms, and tables", but no KIE benchmark (CORD/SROIE/FUNSD) is reported — [Qwen2.5-VL](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.13923).
- **InternVL 2.5**: "Expanding Performance Boundaries of Open-Source Multimodal Models with Model, Data, and Test-Time Scaling", Zhe Chen et al., arXiv:2412.05271, Dec 2024 (Technical Report) — [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.05271). Its DocVQA score, as cited by Qwen2.5-VL, is above.
- **Gemini 1.5**: "Gemini 1.5: Unlocking multimodal understanding across millions of tokens of context", Gemini Team, arXiv:2403.05530, 2024 — [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05530). DocVQA 93.1 as reported in the Qwen2.5-VL table.
- **OCRBench**: "OCRBench: On the Hidden Mystery of OCR in Large Multimodal Models", Yuliang Liu et al., arXiv:2305.07895 (v7, Aug 2024). Overall scores: Gemini 659, GPT-4V 645, Qwen-VL-Max 684 — [OCRBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.07895).
- **Vintern-1B** (Vietnamese MLLM): see Q4.

#### (d) Hybrid pipelines (OCR → LLM with layout encoding, or OCR + image → MLLM)
- **DocLLM**: "DocLLM: A layout-aware generative language model for multimodal document understanding", Dongsheng Wang et al. (JPMorgan AI Research), arXiv:2401.00908, Dec 2023. The venue was not verified (often cited as ACL 2024; the arXiv comment is empty). It is a decoder LLM (Falcon-1B / Llama2-7B) with no image encoder. OCR boxes are encoded as a separate modality through disentangled attention; the final setup keeps text–text and spatial–spatial terms. It is pre-trained with block infilling. Needs OCR. SDDS (same datasets, instruction-tuned) results: DocVQA ANLS 69.5 (7B) / 61.4 (1B); KLC F1 60.3; CORD F1 67.4 / 66.9; RVL-CDIP 91.8. Baselines in the same table: GPT-4+OCR zero-shot DocVQA 82.8, KLC 45.9, CORD 58.3; mPLUG-DocOwl DocVQA 62.2; UReader 65.4. STDD held-out: DocVQA 63.4, KLC 49.9, BizDocs KIE 72.6 — [DocLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.00908).
- **LMDX** (Google): "LMDX: Language Model-based Document Information Extraction and Localization", Vincent Perot et al., arXiv:2309.10952 (v2). Venue not verified. Pipeline: OCR lines plus quantized coordinate tokens go into the prompt with a JSON schema. 16 samples are drawn per chunk, then decoding keeps only values that match the cited OCR segment, which removes hallucinations and gives bounding boxes. Backbones are PaLM 2-S and Gemini Pro. Zero-shot VRDU Registration Single Template micro-F1 76.78 (LMDX-Gemini Pro) vs. 73.62 (Gemini Pro+OCR); Ad-buy Mixed line-item F1 23.29 zero-shot. With 200 training docs: Registration Mixed 92.78, Ad-buy Mixed line-item F1 72.09 vs. 25.46 for the best baseline. CORD: SOTA in all low-data regimes, but at full 800 docs LayoutLMv3 95.66 beats LMDX-PaLM 2-S 94.51 (LMDX's own metric). Localization accuracy is 88–94% zero-shot and 98–99% fine-tuned — [LMDX](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2309.10952).
- **LayoutLLM (Luo et al.)**: "LayoutLLM: Layout Instruction Tuning with Large Language Models for Document Understanding", Chuwei Luo et al. (Alibaba), CVPR 2024 (arXiv comment), arXiv:2404.05225. LayoutLMv3 is the document encoder, its output feeds a Llama2-7B-chat / Vicuna-1.5-7B LLM, and layout-aware instruction tuning plus LayoutCoT is applied. Needs OCR. Zero-shot (Vicuna backbone): DocVQA ANLS 74.27, VisualMRC 55.76; FUNSD 79.98, CORD 63.10, SROIE 72.12. Note that the VIE columns are QA-style ANLS, not entity F1 — [LayoutLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.05225).
- **Name collision**: a different paper, "LayoutLLM: Large Language Model Instruction Tuning for Visually Rich Document Understanding" by Masato Fujitake, LREC-COLING 2024 (arXiv comment), arXiv:2403.14252 — [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.14252). Cite the two carefully.

#### Survey anchor
- "Deep Learning based Visually Rich Document Content Understanding: A Survey", Yihao Ding et al., arXiv:2408.01287 (v2, "Work in Progress"). It groups LMDX as off-the-shelf-OCR-based and UDOP/DocFormerv2 as encoder-decoder. Its performance-analysis tables were not reachable in the fetched portion — [survey](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.01287).

### Inferences
- OCR-based encoders still define the ceiling on entity-level receipt KIE when labelled training data exists: CORD SER around 97.5–98 (GeoLayoutLM 97.97, DocFormerv2-L 97.70, UDOP 97.58, LayoutLMv3-L 97.46). The remaining differences are within OCR-line vs. word-box effects, which DocFormerv2 itself shows by listing LayoutLMv3 at 96.78 vs. 97.46.
- On DocVQA the ranking reverses: general MLLMs (93–96 ANLS) beat fine-tuned OCR encoders (83–88). DocVQA is extractive QA on mostly English typed forms, so this does not show that MLLMs win on line-item KIE.
- Model choice depends on metric families that are not interchangeable: SER F1 (token/entity BIO), Donut tree-F1/TED, QA-ANLS (LayoutLLM), DocLLM instruction-KIE F1 and LMDX micro-F1. A verification-oriented project should use one metric (field-level exact match after normalisation) and re-run baselines rather than quote cross-paper numbers.

### Gaps
- Exact per-dataset KIE scores for TextMonkey (FUNSD/SROIE/POIE), DocOwl2, UReader on CORD/SROIE, InternVL2/2.5 on KIE, and ERNIE-Layout's own FUNSD/CORD/SROIE/DocVQA table were not retrieved. Values often quoted from memory (e.g., ERNIE-Layout FUNSD around 93) are **not verified** here.
- No primary source checked here gives CORD/SROIE/FUNSD/XFUND scores for GPT-4o, Gemini 2.x, Claude 3.x/4, Qwen2-VL or LLaVA-NeXT under a standard entity-F1 protocol.
- Venues for DocFormerv2 (AAAI 2024?), Nougat (ICLR 2024?), Dessurt (ECCVW 2022?), UReader (EMNLP Findings 2023?), DocLLM (ACL 2024?), TextMonkey and DocOwl 1.5/2 were not confirmed (empty arXiv comment fields).
- No technical report checked for Claude 3.x/4 or Gemini 2.x document scores. GPT-4o system card not checked.

## Q2. Head-to-head OCR-based vs OCR-free / MLLM on receipts, invoices, financial forms

### Takeaway
Controlled head-to-heads are scarce. The strongest recent study (SAP, 2026, about 1,000 internal multilingual business documents) finds that **image-only input to strong MLLMs matches or beats OCR-text input** (Gemini 1.5 Pro image-only 76.8 vs. OCR-only 74.1 mean F1). Weaker models depend heavily on OCR (Claude 3 Opus 50.1 image vs. 70.1 OCR). Older results go both ways depending on the comparison. Donut beat LayoutLMv2+OCR on CORD under Donut's own metric, while fine-tuned OCR encoders beat every OCR-free model on DocVQA until 2024-era MLLMs. Line items are consistently the hardest field.

### Cited Findings
- **OCR or Not? (SAP)**: "OCR or Not? Rethinking Document Information Extraction in the MLLMs Era with Real-World Large-Scale Datasets", Jiyuan Shen et al., arXiv:2603.02789, March 2026. Data: two internal datasets (C1 supply chain, C2 finance), about 1,000 docs in total, multilingual (en, es, de, fr, it…), with header fields and line items. Uses an in-house OCR. Metric is key-value F1. Mean F1 (image / OCR / image+OCR): GPT-4o 70.1 / 72.8 / 73.0; Claude 3.5 Sonnet 67.2 / 72.8 / 71.6; Claude 3 Opus 50.1 / 70.1 / 71.5; Gemini 1.5 Pro 76.8 / 74.1 / 75.6; Gemini 2.0 Pro 74.3 / 73.6 / 75.2; Gemini 2.5 Flash 72.6 / 72.1 / 72.2; Pixtral Large 63.1 / 73.3 / 70.4; Llama 4 Scout 68.4 / 68.9 / 68.6. Refining the prompt raised Gemini 1.5 Pro image-only to 78.9. Image-only has the fewest total errors but more "image-to-text" errors (e.g., "I"→"1", "X"→Greek "Χ", "8.00"→"1"). Estimated per-page latency/cost: GPT-4o about 2.2 s / $0.006; Claude 3.5 Sonnet about 4.7 s / $0.010; Claude 3 Opus about 7.0 s / $0.050; Gemini 1.5 Pro about 3.9 s / $0.001; Gemini 2.5 Flash about 1.4 s / $0.0025. Caveats: the data is private, there are no confidence intervals, and the error categories are LLM-assigned. Amounts and dates were not analysed separately — [arXiv:2603.02789](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02789v1).
- **Donut vs. OCR pipelines (receipts)**: on CORD, Donut 84.1 F1 / 90.9 TED-acc at 1.2 s vs. LayoutLMv2+CLOVA OCR 78.9 / 82.4 at 1.7 s, and BERT+OCR 73.0 / 65.5. On a Korean in-house receipt set, Donut scored 78.6 / 88.6 vs. LayoutLMv2 72.9 / 78.0. The paper notes that OCR-based performance varies heavily with the OCR engine (its Fig. 9) and that OCR parameters ("+α") are excluded from size counts — [Donut](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2111.15664).
- **DocVQA, OCR-based vs OCR-free (2023 snapshot)**: Donut 67.5, Pix2Struct-L 76.6 vs. LayoutLMv3-L 83.4, UDOP 84.7, DocFormerv2-L 87.84 (test ANLS) — [DocFormerv2 Table 4](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.01733). By 2025 OCR-free MLLMs reach 91–96 (Qwen2.5-VL-72B 96.4) — [Qwen2.5-VL](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.13923).
- **OCR text → LLM vs. instruction-tuned layout LLM**: GPT-4 + OCR zero-shot CORD 58.3 vs. DocLLM-7B 67.4; KLC 45.9 vs. 60.3 — [DocLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.00908). LMDX (OCR + coordinate tokens + grounded decoding) beats plain Gemini Pro+OCR zero-shot (76.78 vs. 73.62, VRDU Registration Single). On line items, few-shot LMDX reaches 72.09 F1 vs. 25.46 for the best fine-tuned baseline at 200 docs — [LMDX](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2309.10952).
- **DocParser** ("DocParser: End-to-end OCR-free Information Extraction from Visually Rich Documents", arXiv:2304.12484) reportedly finds LayoutLMv3 below OCR-free models on its dataset. This comes only from a search snippet; the paper was not read and the numbers were not verified — [arXiv:2304.12484](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.12484).
- **Vendor / grey literature (low reliability, not peer-reviewed)**: blogs report GPT-4o+OCR 98.0% vs. GPT-4o image-only 90.5% field accuracy on invoices, and Gemini 2.5 Pro 87.46% receipts / 96.50% clean invoices / 92.71% scanned invoices. These are search-summary figures only; the methods were not inspected — [onezipp blog](https://onezipp.com/blog/llm-invoice-parsing-ocr), [koncile blog](https://www.koncile.ai/en/ressources/claude-gpt-or-gemini-which-is-the-best-llm-for-invoice-extraction), [receiptocr blog](https://receiptocr.ai/blog/how-accurate-is-receipt-ocr).

### Inferences
- For header fields on clean documents, strong 2024–25 MLLMs are competitive with OCR pipelines without OCR. Weaker or older MLLMs are not, so "OCR-free wins" depends on the model.
- Character-level confusions (I/1, O/0, Latin/Greek, quantity misreads) are the main MLLM image-only failure mode. These are the errors that symbolic checks (checksums, arithmetic such as line totals vs. grand total, date/ID format validators) catch, which supports a hybrid "MLLM + verifier" design.
- Line items stay the weak spot for every family (LMDX zero-shot line-item F1 around 23). Few-shot plus grounding gives large gains.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed, public-data study found that reports **per-field-type** accuracy (amounts vs. dates vs. line items) for OCR-based vs. MLLM approaches on CORD/SROIE/invoices.
- Latency/cost: only Donut (P40 s/img) and the SAP paper's API estimates are sourced. No controlled on-device latency comparison (e.g., LayoutLMv3+OCR vs. a 2–7B MLLM on Apple silicon) was found.
- DocILE (invoice benchmark, ICDAR 2023) and VRDU were not fetched for baseline tables.

## Q3. MLLMs used zero-shot for KIE (GPT-4V/Gemini on CORD/SROIE/FUNSD, "LLM as KIE")

### Takeaway
Published zero-shot evaluations show generic MLLMs far below supervised SOTA on entity-level KIE. The OCRBench (2023–24) best LMM on SROIE was 41.9 vs. supervised 98.7, and on FUNSD 29.9 vs. 93.1. OCR + LLM zero-shot does better but is still weak on receipts (GPT-4+OCR CORD 58.3). Layout-aware instruction tuning (LayoutLLM, DocLLM) and grounded decoding (LMDX) recover much of the gap. No verified GPT-4V/4o/Gemini-2.x numbers on CORD/SROIE/FUNSD under the standard protocol were found.

### Cited Findings
- OCRBench Table 2 KIE results (accuracy-style metric as defined by OCRBench). FUNSD / SROIE / POIE: Monkey 24.1 / 41.9 / 19.9; DocPedia 29.9 / 21.4 / 39.9; LLaVA-1.5-7B 0.2 / 1.7 / 2.5; mPLUG-Owl2 1.4 / 3.2 / 9.9; supervised SOTA 93.1 / 98.7 / 79.5. GPT-4V and Gemini have **only** overall OCRBench scores (645 / 659), not per-KIE numbers. The paper attributes the weakness to low input resolution (about 224²) and says KIE needed hand-designed prompts. On Chinese, "The LMMs achieve limited proficiency in the Chinese language" — [OCRBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.07895).
- LayoutLLM zero-shot table (QA-style ANLS on VIE): LLaVA-1.5-7B FUNSD 1.93 / CORD 18.06 / SROIE 3.83; Qwen-VL-7B 47.09 / 30.00 / 58.59; Vicuna-1.5-7B with OCR text + boxes 59.63 / 56.13 / 66.20; LayoutLLM-7B 79.98 / 63.10 / 72.12. Fine-tuned LayoutLMv3 reference: FUNSD 92.08, CORD 97.46 (F1) — [LayoutLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.05225).
- DocLLM: GPT-4+OCR zero-shot CORD 58.3, KLC 45.9, DocVQA 82.8; Llama2+OCR zero-shot CORD 13.8 — [DocLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.00908).
- LMDX: zero-shot VRDU Registration Single 76.78 (LMDX-Gemini Pro) vs. 73.62 (raw Gemini Pro+OCR). Its grounding step drops any value that does not match an OCR segment, which removes hallucinations by construction — [LMDX](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2309.10952).
- 2026 SAP study: out-of-the-box GPT-4o / Gemini / Claude reach 66–77 mean KV-F1 zero-shot on real business documents — [arXiv:2603.02789](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02789v1).
- Qwen2.5-VL claims invoice/form structured extraction in its abstract but reports no KIE benchmark — [Qwen2.5-VL](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.13923).

### Inferences
- The OCRBench numbers predate high-resolution MLLMs (Qwen2-VL/2.5-VL, InternVL2.5) and probably understate current zero-shot KIE. Because no verified newer numbers exist, a project should measure zero-shot KIE itself on CORD/SROIE (and a Vietnamese set) rather than cite.
- Grounding the LLM output back to OCR spans (LMDX) is the key published mechanism that turns a generative MLLM into a *verifiable* extractor. It matches a neuro-symbolic verification design.

### Gaps
- Verified GPT-4V/GPT-4o/Gemini 1.5/2.x/Claude 3.x–4 zero-shot entity-F1 on CORD, SROIE, FUNSD and XFUND: not found in primary sources this session.
- Papers such as "GPT-4V OCR capability" evaluations (e.g., Shi et al. 2023) were not fetched.

## Q4. Vietnamese and multilingual (XFUND-style) KIE support and performance on diacritic-heavy scripts

### Takeaway
Multilingual KIE evidence centres on XFUND (7 languages, **no Vietnamese**), where LiLT[InfoXLM] beats LayoutXLM (language-specific SER avg 0.8251 vs. 0.8056; cross-lingual zero-shot only about 0.56–0.61). For Vietnamese, the public receipt benchmark is MC-OCR 2021 (2,436 mobile receipts, 4 fields). Qwen2.5-VL lists Vietnamese in its multilingual OCR training data, and Vintern-1B is a Vietnamese-specific 1B MLLM trained partly on MC-OCR-derived receipt VQA. No paper found reports Vietnamese KIE accuracy of a modern MLLM or layout encoder with field-level, diacritic-sensitive metrics.

### Cited Findings
- XFUND multilingual results (LiLT vs LayoutXLM, average over 7 non-English languages): language-specific SER 0.8251 vs. 0.8056 and RE 0.6781 vs. 0.6432; zero-shot transfer from English SER 0.6061 vs. 0.5561; multitask SER 0.8585 vs. 0.8201 — [LiLT](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.13669). LayoutXLM introduced XFUND (zh, ja, es, fr, it, de, pt) — [arXiv:2104.08836](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08836).
- LiLT works in any language by swapping the text encoder (e.g., InfoXLM, or a Vietnamese encoder such as PhoBERT in principle) while keeping an English-pre-trained layout flow — [LiLT](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.13669). (Applying it to PhoBERT is an inference; no paper was verified.)
- GeoLayoutLM reports no XFUND results — [GeoLayoutLM](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2304.10759).
- MC-OCR 2021 (RIVF 2021): 2,436 mobile-captured Vietnamese receipts. The tasks are image quality assessment and KIE of up to 4 fields (seller, address, timestamp, total cost) — [MC-OCR preprint](https://people.cs.umu.se/sonvx/files/MCOCR_Preprint.pdf), [GitHub system](https://github.com/manhph2211/MC-OCR/blob/main/README.md), [IEEE DOI 10.1109/RIVF51545.2021.9642077 via OpenAlex](https://api.openalex.org/works/doi:10.1109%2FRIVF51545.2021.9642077). The PDF could not be parsed this session, so the winning scores and metric (CER-based) were not verified.
- ReceiptSense / CORU ("ReceiptSense: Beyond Traditional OCR -- A Dataset for Receipt Understanding", Abdelrahman Abdallah et al., arXiv:2406.04493) lists MC-OCR as 2,436 images and 4 categories but labels its language "English & Vietnamese", which looks wrong — [arXiv:2406.04493](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.04493v2).
- Vintern-1B ("Vintern-1B: An Efficient Multimodal Large Language Model for Vietnamese", Khang T. Doan et al., arXiv:2408.12480, 2024) pairs Qwen2-0.5B-Instruct with InternViT-300M-448px and targets OCR and document extraction in Vietnamese. It is fine-tuned on more than 3M image-QA pairs, including Viet-Receipt-VQA. That dataset was built from 2,034 MC-OCR receipts, with annotations generated by Gemini 1.5 Flash — [Vintern-1B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12480v2), [Viet-Receipt-VQA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/5CD-AI/Viet-Receipt-VQA/blob/main/README.md). A third-party (EraX) table lists Vintern-1B-V2 at 37.4 on VI-MTVQA, not a receipt benchmark — [EraX model card](https://huggingface.co/anhbn/EraX-VL-7B-V1.5-Openvino-INT4).
- Qwen2.5-VL's report says it added "a large-scale multilingual OCR dataset" covering languages including Vietnamese, French, German, Arabic, Japanese and Korean. No Vietnamese-specific score is given. OCRBench_v2 zh is 63.7 for the 72B model — [Qwen2.5-VL](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.13923).
- In the SAP study, a non-Latin homoglyph error appears (Latin "X" read as Greek "Χ"), showing script confusion in MLLM image-only extraction — [arXiv:2603.02789](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02789v1).
- OCRBench: LMMs show "limited proficiency" in Chinese, and Monkey does better because of its Chinese training data — [OCRBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.07895).
- A Vietnamese document-analysis survey exists ("A Survey on Vietnamese Document Analysis and Recognition: Challenges and Future Directions", arXiv:2506.05061) but was not read — [arXiv:2506.05061](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.05061).

### Inferences
- Cross-lingual zero-shot transfer for layout encoders loses about 20+ SER points (0.86 multitask vs. 0.61 zero-shot for LiLT on XFUND), so Vietnamese KIE with an encoder will likely need Vietnamese labelled data.
- Diacritic errors (e.g., dropped or swapped tone marks) are invisible to field-level F1 when values are normalised, and very visible to exact match. Vietnamese evaluation should report both, plus a diacritic-insensitive variant, to separate OCR/script errors from extraction errors.
- Training data that is itself Gemini-generated (Viet-Receipt-VQA) can carry the teacher's errors into Vintern. Benchmarks derived from it are not independent of Gemini.

### Gaps
- No verified Vietnamese KIE scores (MC-OCR or other) for LayoutXLM, LiLT, Donut, Qwen2-VL/2.5-VL, InternVL2.5, GPT-4o or Gemini.
- MC-OCR 2021 leaderboard numbers and the exact metric were not extracted, because the PDF was unreadable without a PDF library in this session.
- No study found that measures diacritic-heavy script degradation in KIE directly (e.g., Vietnamese tone-mark CER vs. field F1).
