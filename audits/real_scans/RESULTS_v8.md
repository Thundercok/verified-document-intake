## 5. The text layer does not hold the number or date

45 documents have a text layer on page 1. The parser of §4 finds both a number and a
date in it for 11 (24.4%), 1 of them wrong. In the rest the template
reads "Số:          /2026/TT-BYT Hà Nội, ngày     tháng    năm 2026": the number and the day
and month are added after signing, on top of the page, and are not in the text. On the same
45 documents Vision, reading the rendered page, gets both right for 39 (86.7%).
